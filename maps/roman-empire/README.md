# Roman Empire (map pack `roman-empire`)

The Mediterranean as Rome held it, about AD 117: 45 provinces in 8 regions, carved from Natural Earth 1:10m
countries (`scripts/map/packs/roman-empire/index.ts`). Beyond the frontiers (the Antonine Wall, the Rhine
and the limes, the Danube, the Euphrates, the desert) the land is faint decor: Caledonia, Hibernia, Germania
Magna, the plains east of Dacia, Mesopotamia, Arabia, the Sahara.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `pack.json` | name, one-line description, `order` 2, army room (armies may overhang water: islands, Italy) |
| `rules.json` | seats 2–4, starting armies 43 / 37 / 32, 8 regions, 45 provinces (Latin names) |
| `topology.json` | 72 borders, 11 of them sea lanes, one per strait |
| `scripts/map/packs/roman-empire/index.ts` | the recipe: source, frame, projection, cuts, clip, lanes, labels |
| `board.json`, `thumb.png` | generated by `npm run build:map -- --map roman-empire` |

## Choices

- **Regions.** Britannia (Valentia, Britannia Inferior, Britannia Superior); Gallia (the Tres Galliae,
  Narbonensis and the two Germanies, which were carved from Gaul's Rhine army districts); Hispania; Italia;
  Illyricum (Raetia to Dacia: the old Illyrian customs district, *publicum portorii Illyrici*, which held
  exactly these provinces); Graecia & Asia (Thracia to Cappadocia); Oriens (Cilicia to Cyrenaica); Africa.
  Eight regions, one more than the authoring guide's 3–7: the plan asked for eight and each is a region a
  player can name.
- **Names.** Latin, plain. "Mauretania Caesariensis" is 23 characters, over the 22 limit, so the two
  Mauretanias are "Tingitana" and "Caesariensis", their usual short names.
- **Islands and small provinces.** Cyprus is part of Cilicia (no army of its own, like Ireland in Classic's
  Great Britain). Corsica and Sardinia are one province; Malta goes with Sicilia. Balearica, Creta and the
  Italian provinces are small at true scale, so armies may overhang water (`anchorOverhang`).
- **Lanes (11, one per strait):** the Channel, Gibraltar, Balearica–Tarraconensis, Corsica–Etruria, Messina,
  Sicily–Africa, Otranto (Apulia–Macedonia), Achaea–Creta, Creta–Cyrenaica, the Bosphorus
  (Thracia–Bithynia), the Hellespont (Thracia–Asia).
- **Bonuses** are verify's suggestions exactly (Britannia 1, Gallia 4, Hispania 2, Italia 5, Illyricum 4,
  Graecia & Asia 6, Oriens 4, Africa 2). Britannia's +1 is low for three provinces, but it has one way in
  (the Channel) and is the easiest region on the board to hold.
- **Frame.** The empire spans 12°W–42°E, 24°N–57°N; the frame runs to 26°W–60°E, 12°N–64°N so the faint world
  reaches the screen's edges. Miller (`mercatorLike`) with heights ×0.9, width scaled so provinces are about
  Classic's size; the Nile ribbon stops at Lycopolis (Asyut), so the board frames by width like Classic.
